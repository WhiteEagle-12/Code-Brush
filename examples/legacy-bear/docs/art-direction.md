# The Other Side — art direction and provenance

A gentle 10-second story about apprehension turning into courage. The bear stays visually warm against the cold teal waterfall. Three shots provide an approach, a closer performance beat and a wider crossing. The film ends on the waterfall after the bear has passed behind it.

## Background

Created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this film. File: `films/bear-waterfall/assets/grotto.png`. This file is bundled; reproducing or rendering the film does not call an image model.

Generation prompt:

> Create a production background painting for a 2D animated short film, landscape 16:9, 1920x1080 or larger. NO characters, NO bear, NO text. A magnificent intimate Pacific Northwest forest waterfall grotto in painterly hand-drawn 2D feature animation style, intricate gouache textures, mossy slate rock faces, ferns, tiny plants, warm amber sunlight filtering through dark teal evergreen canopy upper left. Composition crucial for animation: broad nearly horizontal mossy walking ledge stretching from left edge across lower third at y=78% to right-center at x=73%. At x=72% to x=89%, a vertical waterfall drops from top offscreen to a luminous shallow turquoise pool at y=84%, with a dark cave recess behind the waterfall. Waterfall straight down, pale turquoise translucent curtain, quite narrow, not filling whole image. Big flat ledge in foreground left for walking bear to walk rightward INTO waterfall. Right bank rock face is tall and dark with hanging vines. Foreground lower corners richly layered fern silhouettes, small cream wildflowers and stones; keep middle walking ledge clear. Cinematic depth and exquisite drawn rock structure and organic detail, restrained luminous colors, hand-painted animation background, beautiful illustrative artistry. Warm ochre earth on ledge, deep petrol teal shadows, silvery blue water and soft pale golden sun shafts. Avoid photorealism, avoid 3D render, avoid hard vector flatness. Wide establishing shot viewed nearly side-on at bear eye height; no overhead view. Image fills canvas edge to edge.

## Character and motion

Original Bézier geometry authored in `bear.mjs`. Each part has layered color, seeded individual fur strokes and contour accents. Motion comes from tracks, procedural acting and inverse kinematics. No generated video or frame interpolation is used.

## Audio

Original deterministic synthesis in `scripts/soundtrack.py`. No third-party songs, recordings or samples. The included WAV is the rendered score and sound-design mix.
